FAQ

Quick answers

How much does it cost to partner?

Nothing up front. Wis Stationary is free to join — you only pay the small platform charge on completed orders, shown clearly in your earnings summary.

How do I receive payments?

You add your Lipa namba during setup. Customers pay directly to that number through their mobile money app, and you confirm the payment in the portal.

Do I need a website or app?

No. Everything runs through the partner portal — the same login you'll use after clicking "Open partner portal". Works on any phone or computer.

What kinds of services can I offer?

Printing, typing, editing, reports writing, and research writing. You can enable or disable each service from your partner account.

What if I need help?

Reach the Wis team at 0657529385 or email wisstationary@mail.com. We're happy to walk you through setup.
